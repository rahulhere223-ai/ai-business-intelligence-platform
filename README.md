# AI Business Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered business operations platform designed to help
small and medium-sized businesses understand financial data,
automate repetitive workflows, and make data-driven decisions.

## Current Stage

🚧 Active Development

## Vision

Build an intelligent business operating layer that can eventually
connect finance, accounting, operations, sales and other business
workflows through specialized AI systems.

## Current MVP

Transaction ingestion → categorization → financial analytics → AI insights
